Wobbins: Hide & Hue

Online multiplayer · Hide & seek

Hide-and-seek with a twist: paint yourself the colour of the wall.

Play a chunky plush Wobbin that paints itself the exact colour of walls and furniture to vanish in plain sight. The seeker hunts in first person with a paint gun — and every hider they tag joins the hunt. Quick matches, private rooms, camouflaging bots, and a wardrobe full of hats.

DrumHit

Rhythm · Music

The music plays. The drums are up to you.

Built by a drummer who wanted to play along properly, not tap along. Hit every kick, snare, tom, crash and hi-hat yourself, jam freely in Free Mode, or import a song from your device and chart it note by note in the editor.

Tower To Space

Arcade · One-tap

Tap, stack, and build your tower all the way to space.

Blocks slide across the screen and one tap drops them. Land it clean and your combo lives; miss and the block gets trimmed thinner. The climb runs from a back garden, past the clouds and the passing planes, out into the dark.
