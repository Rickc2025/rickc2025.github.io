DrumHit

Rhythm · Music

The music plays, but the drums are up to you.

A rhythm game built by a real drummer. Perform every kick, snare, tom, crash, and hi-hat yourself, jam in Free Mode, or import your own songs and craft custom drum charts with the built-in editor.

Tower To Space

Arcade · One-tap

Tap, stack, and build your tower all the way to space.

Blocks glide across the screen — tap to drop them onto your tower. Nail perfect drops to keep your combo alive, or watch your block get trimmed thinner. Climb from a sunny backyard past clouds and airplanes into a starry sky.

Wobbins: Hide & Hue

Cozy multiplayer · Party

Hide-and-seek with a twist: paint yourself the color of the wall.

Play a chunky plush Wobbin that paints itself the exact color of walls and furniture to vanish in plain sight. The seeker hunts in first person with a paint gun — and every hider they tag joins the hunt. Quick matches, private rooms, camouflaging bots, and a wardrobe full of hats.

About us

Small studio.
Big feelings.

Sonexa Studios is an independent game studio making mobile games with personality. No big teams, no committees — just hands-on craft, from the first sketch to the last sound effect.

Our games start as things we personally love: a drummer's itch to play along with every song, the simple joy of stacking blocks a little too high, the childhood thrill of hide-and-seek. Then we polish them until they feel just right.

🥁Feel first

If it doesn't feel great in your hands within five seconds, it's not done.

🛠️Handmade

Art, code, music, and design crafted in-house, iterated until they click.

🎈Playful at the core

From neon stages to plush hide-and-seek, our worlds are built for smiles.
