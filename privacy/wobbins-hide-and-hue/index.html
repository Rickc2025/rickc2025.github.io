Privacy Policy — Wobbins: Hide & Hue

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Wobbins: Hide & Hue (“the game”) is developed by Sonexa Game Studios. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What the game collects

We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or location. There is no account to create and no sign-up form.

The game does collect the following, automatically:

  • Advertising ID. Google AdMob shows ads in the game and uses your device’s advertising ID to choose ads and measure them. This is shared with Google.
  • Approximate network information. Your device’s IP address is visible to our multiplayer provider (Photon) and to Google’s ad servers, as it is with any online service. We do not store it ourselves.
  • A display name you choose. If you enter a name for your character, it is shown to other players in the same match and is not stored after the match ends.
  • Purchase records. When you buy premium or a cosmetic item, Google Play processes the payment and tells the game what you own. We never see your card or payment details.

Your in-game settings, unlocked items, and character colours are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Children

The game is designed to be family friendly. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at contact@sonexagamestudios.com and we will delete it.

Third-party services

These services operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (purchases) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Photon / Exit Games (multiplayer networking) — dashboard.photonengine.com/account/privacyandcookiepolicy

Your choices

  • Remove ads entirely. The one-time premium purchase stops all advertising in the game.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Delete everything the game stored by uninstalling it. Nothing is kept on our side.
  • In the EEA and UK, you will be asked for your advertising consent choices when the game starts, and you can change them at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy in the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will be noted in the release notes on Google Play.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@sonexagamestudios.com
