Privacy Policy for Tower to Space

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Tower to Space (“the App”) is developed by Sonexa Game Studios. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and almost everything stays on your phone. The one exception is the worldwide leaderboard: if a run is good enough to reach the top 5, the App offers to publish a short name you type plus a picture of that run’s final screen. That is optional — you can decline — but if you accept, it becomes publicly visible to everyone who plays the game.

1. No account, no sign-in

The App does not ask you to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, address, contacts, or location. It does not request camera, microphone, photo library, or location permissions.

2. Information kept only on your device

The following is stored on your phone and is never uploaded to us:

  • Your best score, number of runs played, and other local game statistics
  • A screenshot of your best run, used for the in-game “best score” screen
  • Your chosen language (or, on first launch, your phone’s language)
  • The last name you used on the leaderboard, so you do not have to retype it
  • A list of leaderboard entries you have reported, so they stay hidden for you

Uninstalling the App removes all of this.

3. The worldwide leaderboard (optional)

When a run scores high enough to enter the worldwide top 5, the App shows a card asking you to enter a name. Nothing is sent until you confirm. If you close that card with the “X” button, the run is discarded and never leaves your device.

If you do confirm, the following is uploaded and published:

  • The name you typed — up to 12 characters, letters, numbers, spaces and . - _ only. Offensive names are filtered out. If you leave it blank, the entry is published as “NO NAME”.
  • A picture of your run — the App takes this itself, from its own game screen, at the end of the run. It shows the tower you built and your score. It is not taken from your photo gallery, and the App has no access to your photos or camera.
  • Your score and the date and time the entry was created, recorded by the server.

Each entry is stored with a randomly generated identifier. That identifier is not linked to you, your phone, or any account; a copy is kept on your device only so the App can highlight your own row in the list.

Please do not enter your real name, or anyone else’s. The leaderboard is public. Anyone in the world who plays Tower to Space can see the name, score, date, time and picture of every entry in the top 5.

4. Reporting a leaderboard entry

Every leaderboard entry has a Report button. Reporting an entry hides it on your device immediately and sends us the entry’s identifier and the time of the report. No information about you is included. Reports can only be written by the App, never read back by it.

5. Where the leaderboard data is stored

Leaderboard entries are stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), on Google servers located in the United States. If you use the App from outside the United States, this means your published entry is transferred there. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase’s data description: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Advertising

The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID), IP address, general device information, and interactions with ads, in order to show and measure advertising. See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset the Advertising ID or limit ad personalisation at any time in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

7. How the data is used

The name, picture, score and timestamp you publish are used for one purpose only: to display the worldwide leaderboard inside the App. We do not use them to build user profiles, we do not sell them, and we do not share them with anyone other than the hosting provider named above. Data collected by Google through AdMob is used to display and measure advertising and to prevent fraud and abuse, in line with Google’s own policies.

8. Retention and deletion

A published leaderboard entry stays on the board until it is removed by us or pushed off by higher scores. The App does not currently provide a way to delete your own entry from inside the game. Uninstalling the App removes everything stored on your phone, but does not remove an entry you have already published.

If you want an entry removed, email us at the address below with the name, the score, and roughly when it was set, and we will delete it — both the entry and its picture. You can also use the in-game Report button, which hides an entry for you straight away and flags it to us.

9. Children

Tower to Space is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the leaderboard is public, we ask all players not to enter real names or any personal information. If you believe a child has published personal information through the App, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

10. Security

All data sent by the App travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Leaderboard entries cannot be edited or deleted by other players, and the App rejects entries that do not match the expected format. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
